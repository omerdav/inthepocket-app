## Task: T-006 — Derive matching tolerance from the drill, not from the first two notes

### What I changed
- `src/workers/ScoringWorker.ts`: Changed `maxDistance` calculation from `(targetBeats[1] - targetBeats[0]) / 2` to `Math.max(150, greenWindow * 4)`. This derives tolerance from the drill's stated criteria (R1) with a generous ceiling to catch badly-late hits (R3), and handles simultaneous targets (R2). Also added a tie-breaker so hits with exactly the same delta prefer a matching zone, satisfying R5.
- `src/workers/__tests__/scoring.test.ts`: Added unit tests for simultaneous notes zone preference (R-T1), tolerance scaling (R-T2), and RED vs MISS classification (R-T3).

### User-reachable path
Any hi-hat independence drill (e.g. "Graduation Groove" via the bootcamps menu). Without this, simultaneous targets collapse the tolerance to 0ms and every note becomes a MISS for the drummer.

### Notes for review
For R3, the yellow window is `greenWindow * 1.67`. Capping the match radius at `Math.max(150, greenWindow * 4)` guarantees ample space for RED categorization (between 1.67x and 4x the green window) while preventing hits from matching notes that are completely unreasonable (e.g. 1 second away).
For R5, adding a tiebreaker for `absDelta === absMinDelta` was enough to handle simultaneous hits on simultaneous targets without restructuring the greedy loop into a bipartite match.
