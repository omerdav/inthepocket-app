## Task: T-004 — Left/right hand balance meter

### What I changed
- `src/session/balance.ts`: Created `BalanceTracker` to accumulate running sums of left/right hits with no per-hit allocation.
- `src/session/__tests__/balance.test.ts`: Added tests for `BalanceTracker` to cover balanced hands, weak left, insufficient data, and fatigue over time (R-T1).
- `src/components/drills/BalanceMeter.tsx`: Created the UI component that maps `tracker` velocity differences to a -1 to 1 drift percentage for the marker, explicitly labelling the assumption of prescribed sticking.

- `src/components/drills/DrillSession.tsx`: Integrated `BalanceTracker`, matching incoming hits to notes via existing `startPerfMs` nearest-target logic to pass into the tracker. Added `<BalanceMeter>` above the Rhythm Grid.
- `src/components/drills/DrillSession.css`: Updated `.drill-bottom` layout to cleanly stack the balance meter and the grid.
- `e2e/drill-session.spec.ts`: Added the R-T2 test dynamically making left-hand hits softer and verifying the UI marker drifts left.

### User-reachable path
Start any drill that specifies Left/Right sticking (e.g. Bootcamp -> Dynamics Gate -> Even Single Strokes). Play through the count-in. As you strike the pads, once you have hit at least 4 Left notes and 4 Right notes confidently within the timing windows, the Assumed Sticking Balance meter will appear in the bottom zone above the rhythm grid, updating dynamically.
