## R4: Stick Toggle Feasibility
R4 requests that the drummer be able to toggle Blind Mode by hitting the ride bell and floor tom simultaneously.

**Feasibility Analysis:**
Honoring R4 is currently **blocked**. `StickNavigationController` depends on serial note streams from `midi.ts`. Simultaneous hits (chords) are emitted sequentially by standard MIDI drum brains and arrive asynchronously. Detecting a chord requires time-windowing logic (e.g. queueing notes and resolving them if multiple distinct notes arrive within a 15-30ms window). Since `midi.ts` currently lacks chord detection capability, and we are strictly forbidden from modifying `src/audio/midi.ts`, we cannot implement R4 directly at the engine level right now.
