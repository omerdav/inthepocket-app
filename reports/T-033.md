## Task: T-033 — A silent engine failure leaves no trace

### What I changed
- `src/store/db.ts`: Bumped DB_VERSION and added `STORE_ERRORS` to `ALL_STORES`.
- `src/store/index.ts`: Exported `db` and `STORE_ERRORS`.
- `src/ErrorReporter.ts`: [NEW] Added a central error reporter that tracks `phase`, `drillId`, and logs them with `midiConnected` and timestamp to a bounded 50-item persistent ring buffer in `STORE_ERRORS`. Bounded at 50 to prevent unbounded growth while keeping enough history for diagnosis.
- `src/main.tsx`: Initialised `errorReporter` on app boot.
- `src/components/drills/DrillSession.tsx`: Intercepted returned and thrown errors (e.g. `audio-stall` and `cancelled`) and forwarded them to `errorReporter.logDrillError`.
- `src/components/settings/SettingsMenu.tsx`: Added a "Debug Error Log" option that can be triggered via stick navigation (scroll and select) to copy the JSON log to the clipboard without needing a keyboard.
- `src/components/drills/__tests__/DrillSession.errors.test.ts`: Added assertions to ensure `errorReporter` successfully captures the error state when audio engine stalls.

### User-reachable path
The drummer can view/export the log by striking the Snare Rim to open/scroll the Settings Menu, and selecting "Debug Error Log" with the Snare Head. It will copy the raw JSON of the logs to the system clipboard and display "COPIED".
