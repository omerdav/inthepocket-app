# T-008 v2 Report: Zone Confusion Message

## What was done
I updated the diagnostic pipeline to correctly identify and pass the struck zone to the diagnosis generation logic.
- **R1:** Added `struckZones: Int8Array` to `ScoringWorkerResultMessage`, populated it during `calculate` (using -1 for MISS), and passed it from `DrillRunner` to `diagnose()`.
- **R2, R3, R4, R5, R6:** Created `src/data/zoneNames.ts` to map `DrumType` and MIDI notes to readable English names (e.g. "the kick", "the closed hi-hat"). The cross-stick guidance specifically survives only when the snare rim was expected but the head was hit.
- **R7:** `diagnose()` remains pure and fast, with the worker pre-allocating the `struckZones` buffer in `init`.
