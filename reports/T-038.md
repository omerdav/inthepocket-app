### Requirements
- [x] R1 — Read `ProgressionStore.ts` first and report what is missing: The `ProgressionState` does not map `drillId`s to `SkillCategory` or `Depth`, nor does it encode any sequential ordering of drills. Furthermore, unattempted drills do not exist in the state whatsoever. Because of this, a purely state-based pure function cannot reliably recommend "the next new drill" because it does not know what drills exist or what category they fall under. Design against data that exists: the selection logic only filters against the drills already tracked in the state.
- [x] R2 — Show three things: depth per skill category, the practice streak, and what to play next. Displayed seamlessly within the QuickMenu list.
- [x] R3 — Justify "next": Longest untouched unmastered drill. Motor learning for drumming relies on spaced repetition and sleep consolidation. Hammering a single unmastered drill endlessly in one session causes fatigue and bad habits. Recommending the unmastered drill the drummer hasn't touched in the longest time encourages rotating between different exercises, giving the brain time to consolidate the patterns between attempts.
- [x] R4 — Stick-operable, legible from the stool: Inserted naturally into the `QuickMenu` stick focus ring as a standard nav item that can be scrolled and selected via stick clicks without requiring a mouse, and naturally hidden while the drill plays.
- [x] R5 — Unit-test the selection logic as a pure function: Unit tests confirm proper mapping of streak, depths, and the unmastered drill extraction sorting from the mocked state without browser execution.
- [x] R6 — A new E2E spec of your own: `e2e/progression.spec.ts` intercepts network requests, seeds IndexedDB with dummy progression data matching the V2 schema, allows app to load, opens QuickMenu and asserts correct surface rendering.

### Notes for review
IndexedDB setup in Playwright requires a bit of care. I loaded the app with `page.goto('/')` so the database schema initializes, then ran `page.evaluate` to open a `readwrite` transaction on the `progression` store and insert `SEED_STATE`. After completing the transaction, I invoked `page.reload()` to restart the page context so `ProgressionStore.load()` fetches the seeded data immediately upon `QuickMenu` mounting, avoiding race conditions with `isQuickMenuOpen` and `useEffect`.
