- [x] R3 — Exempted foot zones from dynamic scoring by giving `hihat-chick` an explicit velocityRange of 0–127 dynamically in `DrillRunner`. The decision is that it's the most honest model: foot travel velocity doesn't meaningfully translate to standard stick-grading dynamics and thus they shouldn't be subjected to the same thresholds.
- [x] R4 — `hh-indep-1` passes correctly without any faults.
- [x] R5 — Established that `hh-indep-3` reporting head-instead-of-pedal (even on the newly fixed Perfect Run) is a **matching artefact**. In `hihat-independence.ts`, `hh-indep-2` and `hh-indep-3`'s `sequence` arrays are completely unsorted. Playwright's virtual drummer simulation processes the array in-order using `setTimeout`. Thus it waits out the entire drill dispatching hi-hats, then abruptly dispatches all the snare hits concurrently at the end with timestamps pointing backward to the past. The mismatch arises entirely out of this non-chronological dispatch tricking the ScoringWorker algorithm. Left unfixed (reported here as directed) because drill content definitions are outside R1-R4 scope.
