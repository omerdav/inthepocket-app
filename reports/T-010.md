## Drill 5 Subdivision
I chose **8th notes** (beats 0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5) for Drill 5. The original drill used a 250ms spacing, which is exactly halfway between an 8th note spacing (333ms at 90 BPM) and a 16th note spacing (166ms at 90 BPM). Eighth notes preserve the intended pedagogical structure of an evenly spaced sequence while giving the player enough time to perform the stick changes and dynamic contrasts needed for this Mastery-level graduation gate.

## R6 Test Parameters
- **Allowed subdivision set**: 16th notes (`60000 / drill.bpm / 4`). The test converts `targetTimeMs` into fractional 16th notes and compares it against the nearest integer. No current drills require 32nd notes or triplets. 
- **Tolerance**: `0.1ms`. Since times are computed as `beats * (60000 / bpm)`, small floating-point variations can occur. 0.1ms is enough to absorb float imprecision while being strictly less than 1ms to reject any genuinely off-grid human-authored literals.
